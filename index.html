Chunyu Wang

👋 I am a PhD student in Computer Science at Te Herenga Waka—Victoria University of Wellington.

My research develops genetic programming methods for multi-output symbolic regression. I am interested in improving knowledge transfer, generalisation, and interpretability through semantic information, evolutionary multi-task learning, and reinforcement learning.

RESEARCH FOCUS — Genetic Programming for Multi-output Symbolic Regression

Research Interests

  • Genetic Programming
  • Symbolic Regression
  • Evolutionary Multi-task Learning
  • Feature Selection and Construction
  • Interpretable Machine Learning
