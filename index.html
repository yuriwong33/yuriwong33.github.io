01 / Research

Making machine learning more interpretable, transferable, and useful.

My doctoral research focuses on genetic programming for multi-output symbolic regression. I am particularly interested in how semantic information, evolutionary multi-task learning, and reinforcement learning can improve knowledge transfer and model generalisation.
